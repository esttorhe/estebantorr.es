A blast to the far past

Thanks to [yesterday's post of RandsInRepose](https://news.randsinrepose.com/things-you-probably-need/) I was faced with the reality that Apple is working on the Movie adaptation of [Neuromancer](https://www.google.de/books/edition/William_Gibson_s_Neuromancer/imUREQAAQBAJ?hl=en&gbpv=0).

It made particularly happy despite having some difficulty fully remembering what it was… was it a comic book, was it a book… then it hit me. It was a book I read back in 2011. I really enjoyed it (enough to keep it logged in the back of my mind). I can still picture the green cover (with some Matrix style grid).
![Cover of the book Neuromancer by William Gibson. Shows a skyline with a superimposed Matrix style green grid and the drawing of a face on the right.](/assets/images/til/neuromancer.jpg)

Back then I used to read a lot of physical books. Later on I moved to Kindle and after moving to Germany I fully embraced audiobooks, but holding some books in my hand still carry a heavy core memory.

After remembering all this I went to my `GoodReads` account to see what I thought about the book:

> Good cyberpunk book, really interesting, keep you interested, but its a little bit confusing at some parts due to the excesive use of "cyberpunk" terms.
> Overall a pretty interesting book, highly recomended. 

And there you have it. Esteban from 15 years ago liked it. On a slightly more serious note, here's the teaser from Apple's adaptation:
