Wise words when scoping work

During a conversation with my manager & an engineer where we were trying to understand certain technical decisions made by the engineer; the estimate was 7 days for the leftover work.

After hearing that my manager asked why, their was not following why that specific task would take so long; to which the engineer proceeded to say that they could perhaps lower it to 5 days.

So far very standard, the next part probably very standard for many, I have heard it before, but for some reason this time it blew my mind in a way that got cemented in my brain forever; my manager stopped the engineer and said:

> I'm not trying to reduce time, I'm trying to reduce complexity.

Which makes perfect sense, in their head all bells started to sound and flags waved when they heard 7 days, that means a very complex task, they proceeded to quiz/guide the engineer to a very high level understanding of what's currently existing and what would be needed to achieve the task, and by doing so the engineer was able to simplify their solution. The time was never an issue, but that was a signal that things were being over engineered and being built more complex than they were needed.
