Turn coding agents into teammates

At work I'm currently sitting next to an engineer that lives on the terminal. Their `tmux` setup is a dream. Floating windows, sticky notes, kanban boards; truly pushing the limits of having a `tmux OS` of sorts.

Knowing all this I was super surprised when I saw them on this kanban board with a GUI. After asking they told me that it was called [`Multica`][multica] and is basically a way to organise your agents, have them coordinate the work and you can see the interactions, etc.

![Multica.ai kanban board full of tasks](/assets/images/til/multica.webp)

Didn't take much to convince me to give it a go. I've been playing with it, nice way to organise your work and coordinate your agents. It does have its downs obviously, I've been pushing the limits of it and will write a proper post, but definitely if you have a fleet of agents and want to properly coordinate and see where things are going, give it a go.

The TL;DR version of it is, you define and configure your providers and can create agents assigned to providers and models, that's it. The rest is just a regular task tracker (`Linear` style) that you can then assign to your agents and let them do the work. Basically your team, but for agents.

[multica]:https://multica.ai
