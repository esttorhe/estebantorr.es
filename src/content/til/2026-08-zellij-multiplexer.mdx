Yet another multiplexer (this time in rust)

As a coworker of mine was showing me some new pet project he was building in `Rust`, I couldn't help but notice a nice "theme" on their terminal.

As a curious person, I asked what it was and then he proceeded to show me [Zellij](https://zellij.dev/).

![Terminal showing a split screen with different panes. One big pane on the left, right divided in 3 vertically stacked panes](/assets/images/til/zellij.png)

As an avid `tmux` user I was intrigued. Checking their videos and documentation it seems quite powerful and also "easier" to adopt (it comes with "handholding" as instructions and keymappings are shown on the screen).

Haven't had the time to play with it. With my ways of working very ingrained with `tmux`, `sesh` and all that "controlled" by `Raycast` (in the sense that I have shortcut mappings to switch sessions directly from `Raycast`) the price of assessment and switching seems high at the moment for me; nevertheless looks like a very interesting tool.

And hej, Rust!
